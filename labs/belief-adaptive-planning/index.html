Belief, certainty and adaptive planning

Three small experiments make the distinctions visible: Bayes updates the belief; more certainty need not change an action; and the same one-step values can hide different multi-step contingent plans.

What you should be able to explain

After using all three toys, explain: (1) how an observation updates a belief; (2) why increased certainty may have no decision value; (3) why a policy is stronger than a next action; and (4) why two information states with the same one-step values can still support different adaptive plans.
