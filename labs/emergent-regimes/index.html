Emergent regimes from weak local signals

A complex system can have no convincing story in any single metric pair, yet expose distinct operating regimes when weak signals are combined. Make the pattern appear—then try to break it.

Local absence is not global absence

distinguish

PCA is not K-means

PCA supplies coordinates; K-means proposes a partition. The ARI metric compares that partition with the toy’s hidden generating regimes.

challenge

Variance is not relevance

Increase nuisance variance. PCA may faithfully preserve the largest variation while destroying the regime separation you care about.

Read the mechanism

Each observation is a synthetic system state. Its regime contributes a small shift to every observed feature. Independent noise makes each two-feature view ambiguous, while aggregation can recover the common structure.

xij = weak regime signalij + nuisanceij + noiseij

PCA chooses directions that maximize projected variance:

v₁ = arg max‖v‖=1 Var(Xv)

That objective contains no notion of “correct regime”. K-means is applied afterwards in the two-dimensional PCA space, and may agree—or disagree—with the known synthetic generator.

Engineering transfer

Replace the synthetic features with latency, CPU, queue depth, error counts, network measures, traces, configuration fingerprints, or biometric quality indicators. Candidate clusters may suggest operating regimes, sites, device families, incidents, or hidden batches—but remain hypotheses until they are stable, interpretable, and linked to external evidence.

Boundary. This is a deterministic teaching simulation and a derived pedagogical view. Removing it changes no scientific evidence, gate, or MMALS claim.
