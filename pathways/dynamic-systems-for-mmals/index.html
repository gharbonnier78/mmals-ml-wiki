Guided interdisciplinary pathway

From local structure to adaptive regimes

A guided route through signal reconstruction, local geometry, stochastic and chaotic dynamics, regime inference and the MMALS minimal-sufficient-dynamic-inference hypothesis.

research bridgeadded 2026-08-24
Boundary

This pathway connects mathematical ideas because they may supply useful tools. It does not claim that a machine-learning system is physically a fluid, that latent space is automatically a manifold, or that MMALS is already a world model.

1

Observability and reconstruction

Start with the signal-theory question: how much measured information is sufficient to recover the structure that matters?

2

Local-to-global structure

See how precise local constraints can restrict global behavior without assuming a physical conservation law for MMALS.

3

Local geometry

Replace the demand for one globally simple geometry with locally valid charts, metrics and transition rules when evidence supports them.

4

Stochastic dynamics

Separate explicit probabilistic forcing from model ignorance and from deterministic chaos.

5

Deterministic chaos

A model can be deterministic and explicit while exact long-horizon prediction remains intrinsically fragile.

6

Dynamical regime

Distinguish persistent behavior from a statistical cluster and a causal mechanism.

7

Dynamic compatibility distance

Combine representation, behavior, transition, competence, memory and uncertainty evidence rather than using one latent metric as novelty.

8

Minimal sufficient dynamic inference

Prefer reuse, then adaptation, then fork, and only then candidate regime birth when evidence justifies additional structure.

9

World model

Understand what MMALS would still need to demonstrate before adaptive regime inference becomes a predictive model suitable for simulation, planning or control.

Unifying question

What is the smallest auditable adaptive model that preserves the information and dynamics required to make reliable decisions about an evolving complex system?

Scientific use

Every bridge in this pathway should eventually produce an experiment, an ablation, a counterexample, or a stop condition. A mathematically elegant analogy that does not improve explanation, prediction, control, calibration, cost or falsifiability should remain pedagogical rather than enter the MMALS core.
