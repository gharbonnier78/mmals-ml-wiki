Contingent sufficiency

A project-local hypothesis: a compressed state should preserve the information needed for relevant adaptive decision-making over a declared horizon, not merely reproduce the next action.

project hypothesis · not a field definitionadded 2026-09-01

Intuition

Two histories may recommend the same action now while supporting different future branches. If a representation collapses them only because today's argmax matches, it can destroy information that matters after the next observation.

|V_H^\pi(h_t)-\widetilde V_H^\pi(z_t)|\le\varepsilon\quad\text{for relevant }\pi

Concrete counterexample target

Situation A and situation B both say REUSE now. In A, a probe is nearly useless. In B, the probe reveals whether the next step should be ADAPT or FORK. An action-only compression treats A and B as identical even though their adaptive futures differ.

Mathematical descent

Choose a horizon, admissible policies and a value criterion. Define a representation zt of history or belief. Compare the value of relevant policies using the full information state with the value achievable from zt. The tolerance ε makes the sufficiency claim explicitly task/query relative.

In plain language

The representation need not remember everything. It must remember enough that the decision process can keep adapting when consequential future branches arrive.

Misconception

Wrong: this is the accepted definition of a sufficient statistic or world-model state. Correction: it is a local research refinement motivated by sequential decision structure. Established POMDP, state-abstraction, bisimulation and predictive-state literature must be compared before broader terminology is claimed.

Understanding gate

Explain why preserving today's action can be strictly weaker than preserving finite-horizon policy value, and name at least one declared scope variable—goal, horizon, actions, costs or observations—that can change what counts as sufficient.

Evidence status

Toy F2 is preregistered in the separate research workstream but not yet qualified. This page therefore documents the hypothesis and its intended falsification structure, not a successful F2 result.
