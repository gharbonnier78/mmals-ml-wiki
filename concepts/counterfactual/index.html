Intervention and prediction

Counterfactual

Reasoning about what would or might happen under an alternative action, condition or intervention rather than merely extrapolating the observed trajectory.

general conceptadded 2026-08-30

Important boundary

Action-conditioned prediction is not automatically causal counterfactual reasoning. A learned association can answer “what usually follows this action” without identifying the causal effect of intervening.

World-model relevance

World models are often used to compare possible futures, but the strength of any counterfactual claim must match the causal assumptions and data.

MMALS / ED-POMDP relevance

A future system-engineering world model should label whether a simulated intervention is associative, mechanistic, causal, or merely hypothetical. ED-POMDP is the project-specific Evidence-Driven POMDP programme; GO-ED is only a tentative goal-oriented extension used in this wiki, not established external terminology.

Toy

Construct a confounded two-action environment where observational action-outcome frequencies disagree with the true intervention effect. Use it to prevent “rollout” from being silently equated with causal counterfactual.
