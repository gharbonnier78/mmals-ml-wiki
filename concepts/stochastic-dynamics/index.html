Dynamical systems

Stochastic dynamics

Dynamics whose model contains an explicit probabilistic component, rather than merely uncertainty about an otherwise deterministic model.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-24

Core distinction

Stochastic is close to probabilistic, but more specifically means that randomness is represented inside the evolution law.

dX_t=f(X_t)dt+\sigma(X_t)dW_t

The probabilistic term may model unresolved forcing, random events, microscopic effects or an intentional uncertainty model. Stochasticity does not mean that the deterministic part is unknown.

Not the same as chaos

A deterministic chaotic system can have no random term at all. Conversely, a stochastic system need not be chaotic. Hybrid stochastic-chaotic systems can contain both mechanisms.

MMALS relevance

Noise and smooth drift must not automatically trigger new hosts or regimes. A regime detector should be tested against stochastic perturbations so that architectural growth is not simply a reaction to random variation.
