Dynamical systems

Attractor

A set in state space toward which nearby trajectories evolve under the dynamics, subject to the assumptions of the system considered.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30

Not simply a cluster

An attractor is defined through dynamics, not merely through geometric concentration of observed points. A dense cluster in an embedding does not prove attracting behavior.

MMALS relevance

If MMALS later uses attractor language for regimes or host domains, it must demonstrate trajectory behavior or transition structure, not just latent clustering.

Toy

A double-well dynamical toy shows two attracting regions. Start trajectories from many initial points and observe which stable region they approach.
