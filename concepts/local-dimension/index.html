Local dimension

A measure of the effective number of dynamically active directions or degrees of freedom in a neighborhood of state space.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Local, not necessarily global

A complex system can occupy a high-dimensional ambient space while locally evolving on far fewer effective directions. That dimension may vary across the attractor or across regimes.

MMALS relevance

This supports the hypothesis that host or regime domains may need local representations of different intrinsic complexity instead of one uniform global latent geometry.

Toy

Embed a low-dimensional nonlinear attractor in a much higher-dimensional observation space and estimate how many local directions are actually needed to reconstruct short-horizon evolution.
