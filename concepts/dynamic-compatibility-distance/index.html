Routing and regime inference

Dynamic compatibility distance

A candidate MMALS decision signal that combines several kinds of distance and competence evidence instead of treating one latent-space metric as the definition of novelty.

open hypothesisadded 2026-08-24

Why one distance is insufficient

Two points can be close in latent coordinates but require different outputs or transition behavior. Conversely, visually different observations can belong to the same functional regime.

D_{MMALS}=f(D_{repr},D_{route},D_{behavior},D_{transition},D_{competence},D_{memory},U)

Candidate evidence components

Representation distance, route distance, output/functional distance, local sensitivity or transform distance, transition compatibility, calibrated uncertainty, host competence and memory consistency can all contribute. Their value must be established by ablation.

Decision use

Low incompatibility may justify reuse. Moderate incompatibility with transferable competence may justify adaptation or fork. Persistent high incompatibility across hosts may nominate a new regime. None of these thresholds should be interpreted causally without additional evidence.

Local metrics

The distance itself may need to depend on the local regime or host. This makes metric learning part of the domain-of-validity problem rather than a universal geometry assumption.
