Prediction market

A market mechanism in which trades or score-linked updates aggregate probabilistic beliefs into a shared state.

source-derived mechanismrevised 2026-09-26stable /go/ link

Discover

Participants move a shared probability when their own beliefs differ from the market state.

Engineer

The mechanism is not neural-network training. Liquidity, market maker, timing, information visibility and incentives are part of the system and can affect the resulting forecast.

Research

A visible market state can itself affect participants. Corporate studies also document optimism and correlated trading, so a market is not automatically a passive sensor when traders influence the outcome.

Formal anchor

p_t^M=M(\mathrm{orders}_{\le t};\theta_M)

Study-design boundary

The Engineering Collective Forecasting protocol moved markets out of Study 0 after independent review. Private polling can first test whether distributed knowledge adds information without exposing a social aggregate. A later market study can then test residual market value at the same information time.

Misconception

Wrong: market probability is objective truth. Correction: it is a mechanism-dependent aggregate shaped by participants, information, incentives and market design.

Understanding gate

Explain why showing a market price to an engineer working on the forecasted outcome can transform a measurement into an intervention.
