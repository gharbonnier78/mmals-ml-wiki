Reconstruction from time series

Delay embedding / Takens

A reconstruction principle showing that, under assumptions, delayed observations of a measured variable can preserve the geometry of an underlying dynamical attractor.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30
Y(t)=[y(t),y(t-\tau),\ldots,y(t-(m-1)\tau)]

Why it is interesting

Partial observation does not always imply irrecoverable dynamics. Structure and temporal history can make hidden state reconstructible without observing every internal variable.

Boundary

This is not a universal recipe and does not mean “one sensor is always enough.” The theorem has assumptions on dynamics, observations and embedding dimension.

MMALS relevance

It motivates testing whether compact histories or latent delay states recover transition-relevant geometry better than storing raw full histories.

Toy

Observe only one coordinate of a low-dimensional attractor, reconstruct with delays, and compare neighbor-fate preservation before and after reconstruction.
