Dataset-role separation

A dataset is not defined only by what it contains; its scientific role determines what decisions are allowed after seeing it.

Data-governance conceptreviewed 2026-08-24stable /go/ link

In plain language

If you repeatedly look at the exam answers while preparing, the final exam is no longer independent evidence. Machine-learning datasets have the same problem: the data used to learn, tune, screen and finally judge a method should not be treated as interchangeable.

Engineering view

TRAIN may fit model parameters. VALIDATION may select frozen thresholds or hyperparameters under a declared rule. SCREEN may cheaply decide whether an idea deserves more investment but is not final evidence. Qualification TEST remains untouched until the protocol is frozen. An external-validity reference, such as a sequestered evaluation programme, provides context but may not be locally replayable.

Research view

The role is an information-access contract. Once a TEST result influences model choice, threshold choice or study design, that dataset has effectively participated in development. Preserving roles limits researcher degrees of freedom and protects the interpretation of final evidence.

Formal anchor

data role = allowed information access + allowed decisions after access
Leakage control
Typical roles

TRAIN, VALIDATION, SCREEN, untouched qualification TEST, and external-validity reference.

Misconception

“If I do not train gradients on TEST, I have not used TEST.” False: reading TEST outcomes to choose a method or threshold is still information use.

Understanding gate

Classify which dataset role may support fitting, screening, final qualification and external comparison in a proposed experiment.
