Belief state

A probability distribution representing current uncertainty about a hidden state, updated as new observations and actions arrive.

Why it matters

A belief state can summarize relevant history without retaining every observation. It is only sufficient if the update and state model preserve what the decision problem needs.

MMALS relevance

A future MMALS core could maintain beliefs over hosts, regimes or latent system states instead of committing prematurely to one inferred context. This must remain distinct from route weights unless equivalence is demonstrated.

Toy

In a two-regime sensor toy, plot the belief after each noisy observation. A single observation may be ambiguous while accumulated evidence moves the belief decisively.
