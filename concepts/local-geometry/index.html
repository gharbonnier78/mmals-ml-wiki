Local geometry

A globally complex state space may admit locally simpler coordinates, metrics and rules connected by explicit transition maps.

foundational geometryMMALS application openadded 2026-08-24

Manifold intuition

A manifold can be globally curved or topologically nontrivial while each small neighborhood is described through a local chart.

\phi_i:U_i\subset M\rightarrow\mathbb R^n

Where charts overlap, transition maps tell us how the local descriptions agree.

\phi_j\circ\phi_i^{-1}

MMALS hypothesis

One global latent metric may be the wrong abstraction. Each host or regime may need a local domain-of-validity geometry with explicit compatibility or transition rules to neighboring regimes.

What must be demonstrated

Local metrics should improve competence, calibration, regime discrimination, transition prediction or explanatory value relative to a simpler global metric. Decorative manifold language is not enough.
