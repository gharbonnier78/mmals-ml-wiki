Forecast Contract

A project-local prospective contract that fixes what is being forecast, when it closes, how it will be resolved, which evidence is authoritative, and which safeguards apply.

research hypothesisadded 2026-09-25stable /go/ link

Discover

“Will the release go well?” is too vague. A Forecast Contract turns it into a question that future evidence can resolve without changing the meaning after the answer is known.

Engineer

The contract records outcome type, open/close/resolve times, authoritative evidence, mechanical resolution rule, resolver role, exclusions/void rules, shared context and shadow-mode safeguards. It is versioned and frozen before outcome inspection.

Research

This is a local formalism introduced by the Engineering Collective Forecasting project. It is intended to reduce outcome ambiguity and post-hoc reinterpretation; it is not claimed as an established standard.

Formal anchor

mathcal F_j=(Q_j,Omega_j,T_j,R_j,E_j,A_j,ldots)

Concrete example

Replace “Will performance be acceptable?” with “Will the first valid run of the already-scheduled benchmark satisfy the frozen p95 threshold before T?”, naming the exact authoritative report used to resolve it.

What fails if structure is ignored?

After seeing an unfavorable outcome, an analyst could silently redefine success, choose a different report, or exclude the event. The frozen contract makes these choices auditable.

Misconception

Wrong: precise wording automatically makes a question scientifically meaningful. Correction: a perfectly resolvable event may still be irrelevant, manipulable, or causally entangled with the forecasting process.

Understanding gate

Turn one vague engineering forecast into a binary resolvable contract and identify at least one condition that should make it VOID rather than force a TRUE/FALSE resolution.

Authority and status

Project-local formalism documented in the immutable bootstrap of Engineering Collective Forecasting. See the Diderot source note. No empirical Study 0 result exists yet.
