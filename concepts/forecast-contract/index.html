Forecast Contract

A project-local prospective object that freezes what is forecast, the institutional references, how reality will be resolved and which safeguards apply.

project-local research formalismrevised 2026-09-26stable /go/ link

Discover

"Will the release go well?" is too vague. A Forecast Contract turns the question into an event that future evidence can resolve without changing success after the answer is known.

Engineer

The revised contract includes event/cluster identity, private-forecast timing, outcome semantics, resolution evidence, resolver, base-rate reference class, accountable-owner role, official-status source, exclusions, data custodian and pre-resolution visibility safeguards.

Research

The formalism was introduced by Engineering Collective Forecasting and revised after independent review. Its novelty claim is local: bind probabilistic forecasts to authoritative engineering evidence and same-time institutional references. It is not an external standard.

Formal anchor

mathcal F_j=(Q_j,Omega_j,T_j,R_j,E_j,A_j,B_j,C_j,X_j,S_j)

Concrete example

Replace "Will performance be acceptable?" with a frozen binary threshold, deadline, authoritative report, historical reference class, accountable owner and independent resolver.

Misconception

Wrong: precise wording automatically creates a meaningful scientific target. Correction: a resolvable event can still be irrelevant, gameable or behaviorally affected by forecasting.

Understanding gate

Turn a vague engineering question into a resolvable contract and identify one condition that should reject it during the dry contract funnel.
