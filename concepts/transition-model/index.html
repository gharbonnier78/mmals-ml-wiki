Transition model

A model of how system state changes over time, optionally conditioned on actions, controls or exogenous inputs.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30
p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)

Deterministic or probabilistic

A transition model can be a deterministic map, a stochastic kernel, a differential equation, a learned latent dynamics model or a hybrid. The form should follow the system and the question, not the world-model label.

World-model boundary

This is one of the clearest missing ingredients separating present MMALS routing/adaptation from a substantive world-model claim.

MMALS relevance

A future regime-aware transition model could predict whether the current host/regime remains valid, drifts, or approaches a transition. Such value must be tested against simpler novelty detectors.

Toy

Use a three-regime Markov chain with action-dependent transitions. First learn transition probabilities with known state, then repeat with hidden state and beliefs.
