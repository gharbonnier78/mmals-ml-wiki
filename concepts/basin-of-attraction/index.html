Basin of attraction

The set of initial states whose trajectories evolve toward a particular attractor under the modeled dynamics.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30

Decision relevance

Two states can be close in Euclidean distance but lie on opposite sides of a basin boundary and therefore have very different long-term fates.

MMALS relevance

This motivates testing dynamic compatibility rather than assuming latent distance alone determines whether two observations belong to the same functional regime.

Toy

Plot a two-attractor system and color initial conditions by eventual attractor. Then compare Euclidean nearest neighbors near the basin boundary.
