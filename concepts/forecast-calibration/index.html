Forecast calibration

Agreement between stated forecast probabilities and observed event frequencies across a suitable set of comparable forecasts.

foundational conceptadded 2026-09-25stable /go/ link

Discover

Events called 70% likely should occur about seven times in ten over many comparable cases.

Engineer

Reliability plots need bin counts and uncertainty. Sharpness is useful only alongside adequate calibration.

Research

Calibration is a property of forecast/outcome collections and is unstable to assess in small samples; proper scoring and calibration answer related but distinct questions.

Formal anchor

P(Y=1\mid p\approx q)\approx q

Concrete example

Across 100 comparable forecasts near 0.7, roughly 70 positive outcomes is compatible with calibration, subject to sampling uncertainty. Three such forecasts are not enough to make the same conclusion.

Misconception

Wrong: more extreme forecasts are automatically better. Correction: sharpness without calibration is overconfidence.

Understanding gate

Explain how two forecasters can have similar average Brier scores but different reliability/calibration patterns.
