Control theory

Controllability

A property describing whether available inputs can move a dynamical system between relevant states or regions of state space.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Why it matters

Prediction and control are different questions. A system can be predictable yet poorly controllable, or controllable only over certain states, time horizons or constraints.

World-model relevance

A world model used for planning should distinguish what can be predicted from what can actually be influenced by available actions.

MMALS relevance

For systems engineering, this prevents a learned model from suggesting interventions that lie outside the real control authority of the agent or organization.
