Bayesian filtering

A recursive predict-and-update procedure for maintaining a belief over hidden state as actions and observations arrive.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30

Two steps

Predict: propagate the previous belief through the transition model. Update: reweight the predicted belief using the likelihood of the new observation.

b_{t|t-1}\rightarrow b_t

Boundary

Kalman filters, hidden Markov model filtering and particle filters are different realizations under different assumptions; Bayesian filtering is the broader inference pattern.

MMALS / ED-POMDP relevance

Belief updates provide a disciplined alternative to treating every new observation as a fresh context classification. ED-POMDP is the project-specific Evidence-Driven POMDP programme. The GO-ED wording in this wiki denotes only a tentative goal-oriented extension in which goals, evidence quality and missing evidence also shape the decision; it is not established external terminology.

Toy

A binary hidden regime with noisy observations is enough to visualize the predict/update cycle and show why confidence should evolve gradually.
