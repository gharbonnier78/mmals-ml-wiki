Information and representation

Information-preserving compression

Reducing representation complexity while preserving the information required for a specified prediction, reconstruction or decision.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Preserve what?

Compression quality is target-relative. Preserving classification accuracy can still destroy transition structure, uncertainty, causal variables or future decision value.

Signal-theory bridge

Sampling and reconstruction motivate the broader question of how structural assumptions determine the minimum information needed to recover what matters. MMALS must test its own sufficient-information conditions rather than import Nyquist as an analogy-made-law.

MMALS relevance

The “minimal sufficient” objective should penalize complexity only after defining what information must survive: prior competence, dynamics, regime boundaries, calibrated uncertainty and decision consequences.

Toy

Compress a latent state to progressively smaller dimensions and measure not only current accuracy but transition prediction, regime discrimination and decision regret.
