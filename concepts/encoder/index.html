Representation learning

Encoder

A function that maps observations or histories into an internal representation used by downstream inference, prediction or control.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30
z_t=E(o_{\le t})

Encoder is not state by definition

The output of an encoder becomes state-like only if it preserves information required by the target dynamics or decision. A good current classifier representation can still be a poor predictive state.

MMALS relevance

Host representations, inferred context and future belief/state representations should be distinguished explicitly. The encoder may be shared, host-specific or regime-specific; this is an experimental design choice.

Toy

Train two encoders on the same hidden-regime data: one for current label accuracy, one jointly for next-state prediction. Compare how much transition information each representation retains.
