Architecture

MMALS

A continual-learning research program in which heterogeneous hosts exchange transformed latent signals through an adaptive, auditable medium.

Implemented research programreviewed 2026-09-03stable /go/ link

In plain language

MMALS is a learning ecosystem. Several specialist hosts produce different views of an input. A mediating fungal system decides which signals to transform, combine, preserve, or weaken. Biology is a source of mechanisms and counterexamples, not a claim that nature is optimized or universally mutualistic. Every imported analogy must become an explicit computational object that can be tested.

Engineering view

The architecture separates host encoders g_i, host-specific transformations T_i, an inferred context q(c|x), a router r_i(x,c), a mediated latent state z_f, and a readout. Its key engineering distinction from a basic mixture of experts is that the medium is treated as an active computational layer with memory, costs, contribution measurements, and observability.

Research view

The validated core should remain narrow: inferred-context routing, route-function memory, and energy/stability-aware auditing. Broader claims about biological equivalence, universal continual learning, consciousness, quantum advantage, host ecology, dynamic regime birth, world-model behavior, viability-aware control, path-sensitive adaptation, or emergent mutualism remain separate hypotheses. MMALS is best treated as a falsifiable architecture family plus an evidence discipline.

Boundary

MMALS is not established as universally superior to continual-learning baselines, and the biological analogy is not itself evidence.

Research frontier — minimal sufficient dynamic inference

open hypothesis

On 24 August 2026 a new program extension was recorded: prefer reuse, then adaptation, then fork, and nominate a candidate new regime only when existing hosts are no longer dynamically compatible with the evidence.

The proposed extension combines competence non-regression, calibrated uncertainty, dynamic compatibility, local domains of validity and explicit complexity cost. It must be compared against simpler continual-learning and fixed mixture-of-experts baselines before it can enter the validated MMALS core.

Bio-inspiration design boundary — 2026-09-03

open hypotheses

A new bio-inspiration reading adds four bounded research directions without promoting them into the validated core: viability vs optimization, path dependence, multi-scale adaptation, and mutualism as a normative design principle.

The engineering interpretation is deliberately conservative: preserve declared viability constraints before optimizing within them; retain history only when a current summary is insufficient for the target decision; escalate from reaction to adaptation to structural fork/new-regime nomination only with evidence; and treat “Mutualistic” as a chosen, measurable design property rather than as a law of biology.

Each direction requires separate falsification against simpler baselines. Biological plausibility, analogy or vocabulary does not constitute MMALS evidence.
