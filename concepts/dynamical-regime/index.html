Dynamics and inference

Dynamical regime

A persistent qualitative mode of system behavior. In MMALS, a regime should be treated as a hypothesis supported by dynamics and function, not merely a cluster in representation space.

MMALS use is an open hypothesisadded 2026-08-24

Three things not to collapse

statistical\ cluster \neq dynamical\ regime \neq causal\ regime

A cluster only says observations group under some representation and metric. A dynamical regime additionally implies persistent or recurrent behavior and meaningful transition structure. A causal regime requires still stronger evidence about mechanisms or interventions.

Candidate regime birth

MMALS may use large incompatibility, high calibrated uncertainty, poor host competence and persistent transition evidence to nominate a new regime. Nomination is not confirmation.

Verification questions

Does the candidate persist out of sample? Does a specialized host improve function? Is the split reproducible across seeds? Does it survive alternative representations and metrics? Can smooth drift or stochastic noise explain it without a regime change?
