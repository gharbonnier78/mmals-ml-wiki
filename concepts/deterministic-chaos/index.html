Deterministic chaos

Deterministic evolution with strong sensitivity to initial conditions: a model may be explicit and correct while precise long-horizon prediction remains difficult.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-24

Chaos is not absence of model

A chaotic system may obey a fully specified deterministic law. The difficulty comes from exponential amplification of tiny state-estimation errors.

\|\delta x(t)\|\approx e^{\lambda t}\|\delta x(0)\|,\quad \lambda>0

Prediction can still be useful

Exact trajectories can become unreliable while regime membership, invariant structure, transition statistics or event probabilities remain informative. This distinction motivates risk- or transition-oriented prediction rather than insisting on exact future states.

MMALS relevance

A rapidly diverging trajectory should not be confused with a new task or an unmodeled regime. MMALS should test whether its uncertainty and regime logic can distinguish deterministic sensitivity from genuine structural novelty.
