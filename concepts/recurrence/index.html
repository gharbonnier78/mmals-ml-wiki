Recurrence

The return of a system trajectory to a neighborhood of a previously visited state, enabling local comparisons of repeated dynamical situations.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30

Why it matters

Near recurrences let us ask how states that look similar evolve on repeated visits, providing data-driven information about local predictability and regime structure.

Boundary

Geometric proximity is not enough: recurrence analyses depend on a chosen state representation, metric and neighborhood scale.

MMALS relevance

Recurrence can become an empirical test for whether a learned local geometry groups states with similar future behavior rather than merely similar appearance.

Toy

Generate a low-dimensional attractor, detect near-return points and compare their subsequent short trajectories. Repeat after distorting the representation.
