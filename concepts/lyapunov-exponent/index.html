Lyapunov exponent

A rate describing how infinitesimal perturbations between nearby trajectories grow or shrink under the dynamics.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30
\|\delta x(t)\|\approx e^{\lambda t}\|\delta x(0)\|

Interpretation

A positive largest Lyapunov exponent is associated with sensitive dependence on initial conditions. It limits long-horizon point prediction even when the governing model is deterministic.

MMALS relevance

A model should not interpret trajectory divergence caused by deterministic sensitivity as proof of a new regime. Predictability horizon and structural novelty are different diagnostics.

Toy

Start two trajectories with nearly identical initial conditions in a chaotic toy and measure separation growth. Compare with a noisy but non-chaotic process.
