Stochastic dynamics

Langevin equation / stochastic differential equation

A dynamical equation combining deterministic drift with an explicit stochastic forcing term.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30
dX_t=f(X_t)dt+\sigma(X_t)dW_t

Interpretation

The drift describes systematic evolution; the diffusion/noise term models random forcing under a chosen stochastic model. Stochasticity does not imply the deterministic component is unknown.

MMALS relevance

Langevin-style toys can test whether a novelty/regime detector mistakes ordinary stochastic fluctuations for structural change.

Toy

Run the same double-well drift at several noise amplitudes and measure false new-regime triggers. A robust detector should tolerate within-regime stochastic variation.
