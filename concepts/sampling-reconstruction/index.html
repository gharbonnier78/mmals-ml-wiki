Sampling and reconstruction

The study of when a structured continuous or high-resolution object can be recovered from a limited set of observations.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Transferable lesson

The MMALS-relevant idea is not that every learning system obeys Nyquist. It is that structural assumptions determine how much information is sufficient for reconstruction or decision.

Boundary

Sampling theorems have precise hypotheses. They should be cited as mathematical inspiration, not generalized into universal rules for latent-state learning.

MMALS relevance

The question becomes: which observations, memories or latent coordinates are minimally sufficient to preserve competence and transition structure?

Toy

Compare full-history inference with regularly sampled, event-triggered and learned compressed observations on a simple dynamical process.
