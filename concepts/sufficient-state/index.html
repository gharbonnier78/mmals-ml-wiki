Sufficient state

A state representation that retains enough information from the past for the prediction or decision problem of interest.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Minimal does not mean lossy in the wrong way

A representation may discard historical detail while remaining sufficient for a defined target. Sufficiency is always relative to a prediction, control or decision objective.

MMALS question

Can MMALS retain a compact state of prior learning rather than the full experience history while preserving competence, transition structure and uncertainty relevant to future decisions?

Toy

Give one model the full observation history and another only a compact belief/state. Test whether both make the same calibrated transition predictions and decisions. If not, the compact state is insufficient for that objective.
