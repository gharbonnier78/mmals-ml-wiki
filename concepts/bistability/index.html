Bistability

The coexistence of two stable dynamical regimes under the same overall parameter setting, with state history or perturbations determining which regime is occupied.

foundational conceptadded 2026-08-30

Why it matters

Bistability makes regime inference different from ordinary classification: the same external conditions may support two stable modes, and transitions can depend on noise, forcing or path history.

MMALS relevance

A future regime-aware MMALS should not assume one global parameter context implies one unique behavioral state.

Toy

A double-well potential with weak noise is the canonical teaching toy: trajectories remain near one well for long periods and occasionally transition to the other.
