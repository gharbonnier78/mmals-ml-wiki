Scientific method and pedagogy

Toy model / toy experiment

A deliberately simplified system used to isolate a mechanism, expose a counterexample, test an ablation or make a mathematical idea observable at low cost.

research-method conceptadded 2026-08-30

What a toy can establish

A well-designed toy can provide mechanism evidence, falsify an implication, validate instrumentation or clarify an abstraction.

What it cannot establish

Toy evidence is mechanism evidence, not evidence of general superiority or real-world validity. Scaling, distribution shift, operational constraints and competing baselines remain separate questions.

MMALS history

MMALS deliberately used reduced MNIST-family sequential benchmarks and later FashionMNIST qualification before broader comparison. The pinned public MMALS record explicitly lists MNIST, FashionMNIST, RotatedMNIST and PermutedMNIST, and preserves FashionMNIST evidence packages. Controlled route/function counterexamples, including the v0.9 separation of route, latent and output drift, were also used to isolate hidden mechanisms. This history is not world-model evidence.

World-model program

Keep toys for hidden-regime POMDPs, stochastic-vs-chaotic dynamics, reuse/adapt/fork, local geometry, committor risk and minimal sufficient state. Each toy must state hypothesis, baseline, metric and failure condition.
