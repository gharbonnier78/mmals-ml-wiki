Rollout

A simulated sequence of future states, observations or rewards generated by repeatedly applying a model under a candidate policy or action sequence.

general conceptadded 2026-08-30

World-model use

A rollout lets a system ask what may happen if a sequence of actions is taken. Long rollouts compound model error, so plausibility is not enough: calibration and decision impact matter.

Boundary

A rollout is not necessarily a prediction of one true future. In stochastic systems it may sample one of many possible trajectories or propagate a distribution.

MMALS relevance

Future MMALS world-model work should compare exact trajectory rollouts with regime or event-risk forecasts; the latter may remain useful when exact trajectories diverge.

Toy

In a small stochastic POMDP, compare one sampled rollout with the distribution across hundreds of rollouts. Use the difference to teach trajectory uncertainty.
