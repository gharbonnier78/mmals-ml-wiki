Transition probability

Committor function

The probability, starting from a current state, of reaching one target region before another.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30
q(x)=P_x(\tau_B<\tau_A)

Why it is powerful

The committor predicts transition fate rather than an exact future trajectory. It can therefore remain useful in stochastic or sensitive dynamics where point prediction is fragile.

MMALS relevance

A regime-aware MMALS could compare representation quality by asking whether neighborhoods preserve committor or transition-risk structure better than raw geometric distance.

Toy

In a noisy double-well system, estimate the probability of reaching basin B before returning to A. Compare committor prediction with exact trajectory forecasting.
