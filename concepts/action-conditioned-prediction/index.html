Action-conditioned prediction

Predicting future state or observations conditional on a candidate action, control input or intervention.

general conceptadded 2026-08-30
p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)

Why it matters

Without action conditioning, a model may forecast passive evolution but cannot directly compare “what happens if I do A rather than B?”.

Boundary

Conditioning on action does not by itself establish causality. The model may still learn correlations created by the behavior policy or data collection process.

MMALS relevance

This is a threshold concept for any strong MMALS world-model claim aimed at planning or control. It is not needed merely for continual learning.

Toy

Use the same hidden state with two actions that lead to different transition probabilities. A state-only predictor must fail to distinguish the two futures.
