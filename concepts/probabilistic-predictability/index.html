Prediction under uncertainty

Probabilistic predictability

The ability to predict distributions, event probabilities or transition risks even when one exact future trajectory cannot be predicted reliably.

foundational bridgeadded 2026-08-30

Key distinction

Unpredictable trajectory ≠ unpredictable risk.

Chaotic sensitivity or stochastic forcing can destroy long-horizon point forecasts while leaving useful statistical structure, regime probabilities or event risks.

MMALS relevance

A future world-model-like MMALS should be evaluated on the prediction object that matters. Exact state error may be the wrong metric if the decision only requires calibrated transition risk.

Toy

Compare exact-state forecasting with a committor/event classifier in a noisy bistable or chaotic system. Measure which remains useful at longer horizons.
