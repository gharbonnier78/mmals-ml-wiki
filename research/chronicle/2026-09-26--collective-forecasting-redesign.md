# Chronicle — 2026-09-26 — collective forecasting redesign

Status: **append-only pedagogical amendment**

## Trigger

Independent review of Engineering Collective Forecasting PR #1 returned PARTIAL ACCEPT and kept G1 closed. The main scientific criticism was that the protocol tested a prediction market before establishing whether a simpler private collective forecast added information beyond institutional knowledge.

## Pedagogical consequence

Diderot preserves the Prediction Market concept but no longer presents it as the Study 0 mechanism. The teaching path now separates:

1. private collective forecasting;
2. mean/median aggregation;
3. Brier diversity identity;
4. meta-prediction and shared-information correction;
5. proper probabilistic scoring;
6. prediction markets as a later, behaviorally visible mechanism;
7. the project-local Forecast Contract linking predictions to authoritative engineering evidence.

## New notions

- **Meta-prediction** — a forecast of what other forecasters are expected to report.
- **Brier diversity identity** — exact squared-error decomposition for the arithmetic mean.

## Corrected notions

- Proper-scoring pedagogy now demonstrates expected-loss propriety rather than merely showing that confident errors are penalized.
- Brier score now states the normalized binary convention and its factor-of-two relation to the original two-category sum.
- Forecast Contract now includes institutional references and data/safeguard structure from the redesigned protocol.
- Prediction Market now includes the measurement-versus-intervention boundary for participants who can influence the outcome.

## Scientific boundary

The independent-review simulations are design evidence only, not empirical evidence about the user's organization. Diderot does not promote the project protocol to qualified scientific evidence.
