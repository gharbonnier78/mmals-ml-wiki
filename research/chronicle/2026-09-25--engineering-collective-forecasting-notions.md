# Chronicle — 2026-09-25 — engineering collective forecasting notions

Status: **append-only pedagogical/research event**

## Trigger

A new research repository, `engineering-collective-forecasting`, formalized a shadow-mode protocol for evaluating prediction markets and other collective forecast aggregators as auditable engineering evidence sensors.

## Capitalized notions

Diderot adds six linked concepts:

- collective forecasting;
- prediction market;
- proper scoring rule;
- Brier score;
- forecast calibration;
- Forecast Contract (explicitly project-local).

The first five are anchored in external forecasting/statistical literature. Forecast Contract is anchored in the project protocol and remains labelled as a local research formalism.

## Pedagogical obligations applied

Each concept exposes intuition, concrete example, formal anchor or mathematical descent, plain-language interpretation, misconception and understanding gate. Brier notation is added to the canonical notation registry rather than being maintained as a parallel symbol list.

## Scientific boundary

No Diderot page upgrades the Engineering Collective Forecasting protocol into empirical evidence. Corporate prediction-market results remain evidence about their original populations and settings, not direct evidence for engineering release readiness.
