Validated provenance registry

Datasets, models, protocols and results used by the Siamese Embedding Compression Lab

A source is not identified by a convenient file name. This registry separates first-party authority, validated transport mirrors, exact artifacts, evaluation protocol, observed result, and the personal repository where the evidence was produced.

Artifact-groundedStudy 0 + Study 1Aupdated 2026-08-25

Reading rule

Official / first-party means the source is maintained by the dataset/model authors or their institution. Validated mirror means the bytes actually used in our run were obtained elsewhere and pinned by digest/protocol evidence; a mirror is not silently promoted to official status. The research repository is gharbonnier78/siamese-embedding-compression-lab.

Validated but non-authoritative transport sources

The canonical Study 1A benchmark files were materialized from Icar/val_sets on Hugging Face and were content-addressed by SHA-256 in the result JSON. This is a validated transport source for the exact run, not the scientific authority for the dataset definition. Likewise, the AdaFace checkpoint mirror above is accepted only under the repository's artifact-identity/equivalence evidence; the authoritative model description and published benchmark table remain the AdaFace authors' repository.

Why this registry exists

File names such as cfp_fp.bin or adaface_ir101_webface12m.ckpt are locators, not identities. Reproducible research needs at least: authoritative source, exact transport artifact, digest, preprocessing/protocol, result, and the repository/run that produced the evidence.

Rights boundary

Public availability does not imply unrestricted redistribution or commercial use. AgeDB's official resource is explicitly non-commercial research only. AdaFace code is MIT, while checkpoint/training-data rights are tracked separately in the research repository; this registry does not convert unresolved upstream rights into permission.
