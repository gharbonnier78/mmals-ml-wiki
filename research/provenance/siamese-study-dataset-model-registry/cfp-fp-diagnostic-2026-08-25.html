What was held fixed

All three evaluations use the same AdaFace IR101/R100 WebFace12M checkpoint (SHA-256 0e7a3238d2a50f3fe3860782534928ac7cb2598977cf897f6869fd5ac2493fd0), the same BGR normalization, original+horizontal-flip norm-aware AdaFace feature fusion, L2-normalized 512D embeddings, squared L2 distance, 10 non-shuffled folds, and threshold grid np.arange(0,4,0.01). The published AdaFace CFP-FP reference is 0.9926.

Result

The two independent diagnostic artifacts are not byte-identical to the Icar artifact, yet both reproduce CFP-FP near the published AdaFace value: 0.99243 and 0.99343. The Icar artifact remains far lower at 0.97657 under the same evaluator.

Strong provenance signal
Supported conclusion

The anomalous CFP-FP failure is strongly dependent on the exact dataset transport artifact rather than on a general failure of the checkpoint or evaluation code.

Not yet supported

This diagnostic does not establish which mirror contains the authoritative AdaFace validation bytes, does not prove that the canonical Icar artifact is intrinsically “wrong”, and does not automatically change Study 1A from FAIL to PASS.

Canonical semantics

The canonical Study 1A decision remains FAIL because the preregistered run used the Icar artifact. Diagnostic evidence can challenge the validity of that canonical object, but a new canonical result requires a separately reviewed provenance correction and replay. This is the practical meaning of canonical research object: currently authorized reference for a bounded decision, not absolute truth.
